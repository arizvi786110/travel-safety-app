## Data and APIs

- **Perigon**: news articles used for safety ratings
- **Open-Meteo**: weather data and city geocoding
- **Google Maps Platform**: Places and Distance Matrix
- **SerpApi**: flight price results
- **OpenFlights**: airport and route data used to build the flight graph
- **OpenWeather**: weather data

## AI

- **Groq**: model inference for the assistant
- **OpenAI**: the open-weight `gpt-oss` model
- **LangChain**: tooling for the assistant pipeline

## Infrastructure

- **Supabase**: database and authentication
- **Expo** and **EAS Hosting**: app framework, builds and hosting
- **Resend**: transactional email
- **IONOS**: domain registration
